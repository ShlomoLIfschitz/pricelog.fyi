# Destination photos

One photo per destination, from Wikimedia Commons, chosen by hand on
2026-10-07 from its Featured and Quality images. Each is served at three widths
(`<code>-800.webp`, `-1280.webp`, `-1920.webp`), scaled down and re-encoded
from the Commons original; nothing else is changed.

Most of these licences require attribution, and the share-alike ones require
the scaled copies here to carry the same licence, which they do. Every page that
shows a photo credits it, and `/he/credits` lists all of them. Adding a photo
means adding its row here and its credit there, in the same commit.
