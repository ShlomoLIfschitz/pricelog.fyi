Luchot

Privacy Policy

Luchot is a Hebrew/Gregorian calendar app for Android. This page describes what happens to information about you in the app. עברית

Who develops the app

Luchot is developed by Shlomo Lifschitz, a private individual in Israel. For any privacy question or request: shlomo.apps.dev@gmail.com.

Summary

Luchot runs on your device. It has no user account, no server of mine, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter (events, notes, reminders and settings) is stored only on your device, and I never receive it.

Data stored on your device

  • Events, notes, reminders and settings are stored in a local database inside the app.
  • The location you choose for zmanim (a city or coordinates) is stored on the device.
  • None of this is sent to me. If your Android device backs up apps to your Google Account (Android Auto Backup), that backup is handled by Google under your device settings, not by me.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from the device.

Permissions

  • Location: only if you choose “current location”, to calculate zmanim (halachic times) for where you are, or to tag a note with a place. You can use the app without it by picking a city from a list.
  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set. Reminders are scheduled on the device itself.
  • Exact alarms: so a reminder appears at the exact time you set.

When the app connects to the internet

The app sends no data to me. It contacts an outside service in two cases:

  • Place names from location. When you ask for your current location and no known city is nearby, or when you tag a note with your location, the app sends coordinates rounded to about 110 meters to OpenStreetMap’s Nominatim service to get a place name. Nothing else is sent, and this happens only in response to your action, never in the background. The service receives your IP address, as any server you contact does, under the OSMF privacy policy.
  • App updates. When the app opens it checks for a software update through the Expo update service (Expo privacy policy). The check carries technical details only, such as the app and OS version and a random installation identifier, and none of the content you entered.

External links in the app (for example to Sefaria) open in your browser, under that site’s own policy.

Import and export

Exporting to and importing from .ics files happens on the device. A file goes only where you choose to share it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from any user.

Your rights

Under Israel’s Privacy Protection Law you may access information held about you and ask to correct or delete it. I hold no information about you from the app, so in practice this covers only email correspondence, if you write to me. I will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
