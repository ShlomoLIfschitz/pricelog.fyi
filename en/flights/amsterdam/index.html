Questions & answers

Flights to Amsterdam: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Amsterdam, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Amsterdam?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Amsterdam is $355, based on 2,392 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $265.

One way, the median is $143 and the lowest 10% are under $98 (978 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Amsterdam?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $313, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Prices have been collected since 11 Sep 2026, so they do not cover a full year. Months with fewer than 200 prices are not shown. Each month's prices were collected at a different distance from departure.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Amsterdam?

The prices collected include direct flights: 167 of 2,392 round-trip prices and 75 of 978 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Amsterdam?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Amsterdam takes about 5 hr. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Amsterdam and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.
