About the destination

Belgrade, Serbia

The capital of Serbia, where the Sava River meets the Danube. Kalemegdan Fortress overlooks the confluence, and the Temple of Saint Sava is one of the largest Orthodox churches in the world. Zemun, on the bank of the Danube, was a separate town until 1934.
