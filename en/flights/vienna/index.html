Questions & answers

Flights to Vienna: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Vienna, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Vienna?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Vienna is $344, based on 1,470 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $265.

One way, the median is $133 and the lowest 10% are under $98 (572 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Vienna?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $312, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Vienna?

The prices collected include direct flights: 171 of 1,470 round-trip prices and 42 of 572 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Vienna and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Vienna, Austria

The capital of Austria, on the Danube. The historic center, with St. Stephen's Cathedral and the Hofburg, is a World Heritage Site, and so are Schonbrunn Palace and its gardens, inside the city.
