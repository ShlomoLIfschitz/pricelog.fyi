Questions & answers

Flights to Barcelona: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Barcelona, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Barcelona?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Barcelona is $244, based on 2,014 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $198.

One way, the median is $105 and the lowest 10% are under $81 (1,091 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Barcelona?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $213, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $91.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Barcelona?

The prices collected include direct flights: 18 of 2,014 round-trip prices and 29 of 1,091 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Barcelona?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Barcelona takes about 4 hr 45 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Barcelona and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.
