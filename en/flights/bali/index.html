Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Bali and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Bali, Indonesia

An island and province of Indonesia, east of Java. Most of its people are Hindu, unlike the rest of the country, which is mostly Muslim. The subak, the traditional irrigation system of its rice fields, is a World Heritage Site, and Ngurah Rai Airport is in the south of the island, near its capital, Denpasar.
