The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Antalya is $382, based on 1,027 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $320.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Antalya?

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Antalya and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.
