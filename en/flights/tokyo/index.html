How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Tokyo?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Tokyo is $1,070, based on 1,683 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $875.

One way, the median is $599 and the lowest 10% are under $498 (633 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Tokyo?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $1,106, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Tokyo and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Tokyo, Japan

Japan's capital has two international airports: Haneda, inside the city, and Narita, about an hour's drive east, in Chiba Prefecture. Tokyo Tower opened in 1958 and is 1,093 feet tall, and Tokyo Skytree, at 2,080 feet, opened in 2012. The National Museum of Western Art in Ueno Park, designed by Le Corbusier, is listed as a World Heritage Site.
