Questions & answers

Flights to Palermo: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Palermo, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Palermo?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Palermo is $147, based on 334 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $87.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Palermo?

The prices collected include direct flights: 176 of 334 round-trip prices and 66 of 116 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Palermo and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Palermo, Italy

The capital of Sicily, on the island's north coast. The cathedral and the Norman Palace, with its Palatine Chapel, are part of the World Heritage Site "Arab-Norman Palermo". Teatro Massimo, opened in 1897, is the largest opera house in Italy.
