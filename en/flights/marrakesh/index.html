How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Marrakesh?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Marrakesh is $257, based on 278 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $200.

One way, the median is $121 and the lowest 10% are under $89 (329 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Marrakesh?

The prices collected include direct flights: 0 of 278 round-trip prices and 1 of 329 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Marrakesh and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Marrakesh, Morocco

A city in Morocco near the foothills of the Atlas Mountains, founded in the 11th century. Its old town, the medina, is a World Heritage Site and holds the Jemaa el-Fnaa square. The minaret of the Koutoubia Mosque, from the 12th century, is about 250 feet tall.
