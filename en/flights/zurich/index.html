Questions & answers

Flights to Zurich: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Zurich, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Zurich?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Zurich is $325, based on 295 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $264.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Zurich?

The prices collected include direct flights: 68 of 295 round-trip prices and 9 of 168 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Zurich and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Zurich, Switzerland

The largest city in Switzerland, at the north end of Lake Zurich, where the Limmat River flows out of it. The Fraumunster church has stained-glass windows by Marc Chagall, and the city's airport is the largest in Switzerland.
