About the destination

Prague, Czech Republic

Charles Bridge, Prague Castle and the Old Town with its astronomical clock, all within the World Heritage Site of the historic center. In the Jewish Quarter, Josefov, are the Old Jewish Cemetery and the Old-New Synagogue, built in the 13th century and still in use for prayer.
