Questions & answers

Flights to Tirana: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Tirana, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Tirana?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Tirana is $263, based on 228 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $194.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Tirana?

The prices collected include direct flights: 22 of 228 round-trip prices and 16 of 91 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Tirana and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Tirana, Albania

The capital of Albania since 1920. On Skanderbeg Square, in the city center, stand the statue of the national hero and the Et'hem Bey Mosque, completed in 1821. The Dajti cable car climbs from the edge of the city to Mount Dajti.
