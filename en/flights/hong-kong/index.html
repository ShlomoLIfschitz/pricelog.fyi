Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Hong Kong and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Hong Kong, China

A special administrative region of China since 1997, and a British colony before that. Victoria Harbour separates Hong Kong Island from the Kowloon peninsula, and Victoria Peak, the highest point on the island, rises about 1,810 feet. The airport is on the island of Chek Lap Kok and opened in 1998.
