How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Beijing?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Beijing is $1,003, based on 295 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $801.

One way, the median is $502 and the lowest 10% are under $363 (241 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Beijing?

None of the 536 prices collected for Beijing were for a direct flight.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Beijing and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Beijing, China

The capital of China. The city has two international airports: Beijing Capital, and Daxing, which opened in 2019. The Forbidden City, the palace of the Ming and Qing emperors, is a World Heritage Site, as are the Temple of Heaven and the Summer Palace.
