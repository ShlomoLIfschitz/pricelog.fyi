Flights to Bucharest: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Bucharest, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Bucharest?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Bucharest is $147, based on 1,254 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $133.

One way, the median is $77 and the lowest 10% are under $67 (241 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Bucharest?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $140, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Bucharest?

The prices collected include direct flights: 1,028 of 1,254 round-trip prices and 217 of 241 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $144.

One way, the median of direct flights is $75.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Bucharest and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Bucharest, Romania

The capital of Romania. The Palace of the Parliament, one of the largest administrative buildings in the world, stands at the end of Unirii Boulevard. Along Calea Victoriei are the CEC Palace, completed in 1900, the Romanian Athenaeum and the National Museum of Art.
