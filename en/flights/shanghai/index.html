Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Shanghai and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Shanghai, China

Shanghai lies on the estuary of the Yangtze River. Shanghai Tower, in the Pudong district, rises 2,073 feet and is the tallest building in China, and opposite it, on the other bank of the Huangpu River, runs the Bund promenade. The Ohel Moshe Synagogue in the Hongkou district houses a museum of the about 20,000 Jewish refugees who found refuge in the city in the 1930s and 1940s.
