Questions & answers

Flights to New York: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for New York, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to New York?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to New York is $817, based on 1,671 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $720.

One way, the median is $469 and the lowest 10% are under $405 (749 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to New York?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $764, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Prices have been collected since 11 Oct 2026, so they do not cover a full year. Months with fewer than 200 prices are not shown. Each month's prices were collected at a different distance from departure.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to New York?

The prices collected include direct flights: 96 of 1,671 round-trip prices and 218 of 749 one-way prices.

One way, the median of direct flights is $418.

Round trip, fewer than 200 direct prices were collected, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to New York and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

New York, United States

The largest city in the United States is divided into five boroughs, and Manhattan is the most densely populated of them. The Statue of Liberty is a World Heritage Site, and the Guggenheim Museum is one of eight Frank Lloyd Wright buildings listed together as another. Central Park, in the middle of Manhattan, covers about 840 acres.
