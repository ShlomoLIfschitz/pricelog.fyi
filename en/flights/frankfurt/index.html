Questions & answers

Flights to Frankfurt: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Frankfurt, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Frankfurt?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Frankfurt is $280, based on 363 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $216.

One way, the median is $111 and the lowest 10% are under $80 (318 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Frankfurt?

The prices collected include direct flights: 2 of 363 round-trip prices and 21 of 318 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Frankfurt and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Frankfurt, Germany

Frankfurt, on the Main River, is the seat of the European Central Bank, and its airport is the largest in Germany. Johann Wolfgang von Goethe was born in the city in 1749, and the house he was born in is open as a museum.
