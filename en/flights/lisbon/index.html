Questions & answers

Flights to Lisbon: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Lisbon, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Lisbon?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Lisbon is $345, based on 941 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $249.

One way, the median is $149 and the lowest 10% are under $105 (616 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Lisbon?

The prices collected include direct flights: 18 of 941 round-trip prices and 16 of 616 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Lisbon and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Lisbon, Portugal

The capital of Portugal sits on the estuary of the Tagus River, a few miles from the Atlantic Ocean. In the Belem district, in the west of the city, are Belem Tower and the Jeronimos Monastery, listed as a World Heritage Site. Tram 28 runs through the lanes of Alfama, the city's old quarter.
