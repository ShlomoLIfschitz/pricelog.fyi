How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Geneva?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Geneva is $320, based on 392 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $245.

One way, the median is $121 and the lowest 10% are under $101 (230 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Geneva?

The prices collected include direct flights: 4 of 392 round-trip prices and 0 of 230 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Geneva and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Geneva, Switzerland

A city in southwestern Switzerland, at the end of Lake Geneva where the Rhone flows out of it. The United Nations Office at Geneva is in the Palais des Nations, and the International Committee of the Red Cross has its headquarters in the city. The Jet d'Eau fountain rises about 460 feet, and CERN is on the city's outskirts.
