Questions & answers

Flights to Belgrade: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Belgrade, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Belgrade?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Belgrade is $274, based on 1,172 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $211.

One way, the median is $132 and the lowest 10% are under $93 (1,099 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Belgrade?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $259, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is December 2026, with a median of $115.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Belgrade?

The prices collected include direct flights: 25 of 1,172 round-trip prices and 51 of 1,099 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Belgrade and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Belgrade, Serbia

The capital of Serbia, where the Sava River meets the Danube. Kalemegdan Fortress overlooks the confluence, and the Temple of Saint Sava is one of the largest Orthodox churches in the world. Zemun, on the bank of the Danube, was a separate town until 1934.
