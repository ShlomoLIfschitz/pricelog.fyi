Questions & answers

Flights to Naples: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Naples, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Naples?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Naples is $141, based on 1,194 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $100.

One way, the median is $68 and the lowest 10% are under $51 (447 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Naples?

The prices collected include direct flights: 798 of 1,194 round-trip prices and 310 of 447 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $110.

One way, the median of direct flights is $58.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Naples?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Naples takes about 3 hr 30 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Naples and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Naples, Italy

The historic center is a World Heritage Site, and Mount Vesuvius rises above the bay. Pompeii and Herculaneum are a train ride away, and ferries to Capri and Ischia leave from the port. The art of the Neapolitan pizzaiuolo has been on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list since 2017.

  • Direct flight: about 3 hr 30 min
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
