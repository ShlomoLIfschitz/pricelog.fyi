Questions & answers

Flights to Prague: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Prague, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Prague?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Prague is $294, based on 4,746 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $216.

One way, the median is $117 and the lowest 10% are under $93 (1,586 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Prague?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $240, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $107.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Prague?

The prices collected include direct flights: 326 of 4,746 round-trip prices and 283 of 1,586 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $309.

One way, the median of direct flights is $125.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Prague?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Prague takes about 4 hr. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Prague and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Prague, Czech Republic

Charles Bridge, Prague Castle and the Old Town with its astronomical clock, all within the World Heritage Site of the historic center. In the Jewish Quarter, Josefov, are the Old Jewish Cemetery and the Old-New Synagogue, built in the 13th century and still in use for prayer.

  • Direct flight: about 4 hr
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
