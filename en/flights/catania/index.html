How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Catania?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Catania is $162, based on 791 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $94.

One way, the median is $81 and the lowest 10% are under $45 (405 prices).

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $109, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Catania?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Catania takes about 3 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Catania and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Catania, Sicily, Italy

Sicily's second-largest city, at the foot of Mount Etna. Its center was rebuilt in Baroque style after the 1693 earthquake, and many of its buildings are made of dark volcanic stone. It is part of the World Heritage Site of the Late Baroque Towns of the Val di Noto. Taormina is about an hour's drive north.

  • Direct flight: about 3 hr 15 min
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
