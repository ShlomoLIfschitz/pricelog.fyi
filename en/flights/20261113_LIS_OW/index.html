About the destination

Lisbon, Portugal

The capital of Portugal sits on the estuary of the Tagus River, a few miles from the Atlantic Ocean. In the Belem district, in the west of the city, are Belem Tower and the Jeronimos Monastery, listed as a World Heritage Site. Tram 28 runs through the lanes of Alfama, the city's old quarter.
