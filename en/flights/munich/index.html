Questions & answers

Flights to Munich: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Munich, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Munich?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Munich is $371, based on 594 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $310.

One way, the median is $156 and the lowest 10% are under $123 (310 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Munich?

The prices collected include direct flights: 47 of 594 round-trip prices and 43 of 310 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Munich and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Munich, Germany

The capital of the state of Bavaria. The New Town Hall stands on Marienplatz, the main square, and its tower holds the Glockenspiel. Oktoberfest is held on the Theresienwiese, and the Dachau concentration camp memorial site is about 12 miles northwest of the city.
