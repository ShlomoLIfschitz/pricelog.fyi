Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Colombo and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Colombo, Sri Lanka

The largest city in Sri Lanka and its commercial capital, on the island's west coast. The official capital, Sri Jayawardenepura Kotte, borders it. The Colombo National Museum opened in 1877, and Bandaranaike International Airport is in Katunayake, about 20 miles north of the city.
