Questions & answers

Flights to Yerevan: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Yerevan, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Yerevan?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Yerevan is $244, based on 3,879 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $170.

One way, the median is $119 and the lowest 10% are under $79 (3,685 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Yerevan?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $185, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is December 2026, with a median of $102.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Yerevan?

The prices collected include direct flights: 50 of 3,879 round-trip prices and 848 of 3,685 one-way prices.

One way, the median of direct flights is $104.

Round trip, fewer than 200 direct prices were collected, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Yerevan?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Yerevan takes about 2 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Yerevan and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Yerevan, Armenia

The capital of Armenia, built largely of pink-toned tuff stone, with Mount Ararat visible on the horizon on clear days. The Cascade steps are in the city center. Etchmiadzin Cathedral and Geghard Monastery, both World Heritage Sites, are a drive away.
