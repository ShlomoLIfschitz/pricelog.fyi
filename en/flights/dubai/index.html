Questions & answers

Flights to Dubai: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Dubai, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Dubai?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Dubai is $466, based on 939 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $270.

One way, the median is $244 and the lowest 10% are under $131 (707 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Dubai?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in December 2026, the median is $474, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Prices have been collected since 11 Oct 2026, so they do not cover a full year. Months with fewer than 200 prices are not shown. Each month's prices were collected at a different distance from departure.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Dubai?

The prices collected include direct flights: 540 of 939 round-trip prices and 356 of 707 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $369.

One way, the median of direct flights is $168.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Dubai and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Dubai, United Arab Emirates

The largest city in the United Arab Emirates, on the coast of the Persian Gulf. The Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world, stands downtown, and the Burj Al Arab hotel on an artificial island off Jumeirah Beach. Dubai Creek runs through the old city, and wooden boats ferry passengers from one bank to the other.
