About the destination

Dubai, United Arab Emirates

The largest city in the United Arab Emirates, on the coast of the Persian Gulf. The Burj Khalifa, the tallest building in the world, stands downtown, and the Burj Al Arab hotel on an artificial island off Jumeirah Beach. Dubai Creek runs through the old city, and wooden boats ferry passengers from one bank to the other.
