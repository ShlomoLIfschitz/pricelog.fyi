Questions & answers

Flights to Rhodes: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Rhodes, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Rhodes?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Rhodes is $261, based on 605 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $161.

One way, the median is $107 and the lowest 10% are under $79 (236 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Rhodes?

The prices collected include direct flights: 387 of 605 round-trip prices and 183 of 236 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $224.

One way, fewer than 200 direct prices were collected, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Rhodes?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Rhodes takes about 1 hr 45 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Rhodes and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Rhodes, Greece

The medieval city of Rhodes, walled by the Knights Hospitaller, is a World Heritage Site. On the island's east coast is the village of Lindos, below its acropolis.
