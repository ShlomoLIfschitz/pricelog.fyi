Questions & answers

Flights to Thessaloniki: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Thessaloniki, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Thessaloniki?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Thessaloniki is $265, based on 367 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $199.

One way, the median is $102 and the lowest 10% are under $70 (244 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Thessaloniki?

The prices collected include direct flights: 103 of 367 round-trip prices and 132 of 244 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Thessaloniki?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Thessaloniki takes about 2 hr 30 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Thessaloniki and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Thessaloniki, Greece

Greece's second-largest city, on the Thermaic Gulf: a promenade along the water, the White Tower, and Early Christian and Byzantine monuments, churches among them, that form a World Heritage Site. The Halkidiki peninsula lies southeast of the city.
