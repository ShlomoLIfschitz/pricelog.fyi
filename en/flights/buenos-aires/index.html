Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Buenos Aires and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Buenos Aires, Argentina

The capital of Argentina, on the Rio de la Plata. The Casa Rosada, the president's office building, stands on Plaza de Mayo, and the Teatro Colon opened in 1908. The city has the largest Jewish community in Latin America.
