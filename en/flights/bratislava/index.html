Questions & answers

Flights to Bratislava: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Bratislava, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Bratislava?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Bratislava is $172, based on 486 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $141.

One way, the median is $89 and the lowest 10% are under $72 (239 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Bratislava?

The prices collected include direct flights: 314 of 486 round-trip prices and 147 of 239 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $151.

One way, fewer than 200 direct prices were collected, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Bratislava and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Bratislava, Slovakia

The capital of Slovakia, on the Danube River, near the borders with Austria and Hungary. The kings of Hungary were crowned in St. Martin's Cathedral from 1563 to 1830. Vienna is about 35 miles to the west.
