Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Goa and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Goa, India

The smallest state in India by area, on the west coast. Goa was a Portuguese colony until 1961, and the churches and convents of Old Goa, among them the Basilica of Bom Jesus, are a World Heritage Site. Goa has two airports: Dabolim, and Manohar in Mopa, which opened in 2023.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
