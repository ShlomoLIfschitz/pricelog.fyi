Questions & answers

Flights to Berlin: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Berlin, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Berlin?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Berlin is $284, based on 2,826 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $235.

One way, the median is $124 and the lowest 10% are under $98 (1,527 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Berlin?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $262, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $112.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Berlin?

The prices collected include direct flights: 125 of 2,826 round-trip prices and 184 of 1,527 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Berlin?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Berlin takes about 4 hr 30 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Berlin and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.
