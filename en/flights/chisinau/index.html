Questions & answers

Flights to Chisinau: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Chisinau, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Chisinau?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Chisinau is $196, based on 1,533 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $138.

One way, the median is $101 and the lowest 10% are under $81 (835 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Chisinau?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $189, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $101.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Chisinau?

The prices collected include direct flights: 79 of 1,533 round-trip prices and 176 of 835 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Chisinau and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Chisinau, Moldova

The capital of Moldova. On Stephen the Great Boulevard are the park with the prince's statue from 1928, the Great National Assembly Square, the Triumphal Arch and the Nativity Cathedral. About 12 miles south of the city, the Milestii Mici wine cellars, which Guinness World Records lists as the largest wine collection in the world.
