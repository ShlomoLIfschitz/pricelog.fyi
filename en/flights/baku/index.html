Questions & answers

Flights to Baku: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Baku, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Baku?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Baku is $285, based on 1,399 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $220.

One way, the median is $134 and the lowest 10% are under $114 (1,021 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Baku?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $249, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $130.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Baku?

The prices collected include direct flights: 1,081 of 1,399 round-trip prices and 819 of 1,021 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $265.

One way, the median of direct flights is $131.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Baku and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Baku, Azerbaijan

The capital of Azerbaijan, on the Caspian Sea coast. The walled city, with the Palace of the Shirvanshahs and the Maiden Tower, is a World Heritage Site. The three Flame Towers stand on the hill above it, and the seaside boulevard runs along the bay.
