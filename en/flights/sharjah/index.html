Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Sharjah and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Sharjah, United Arab Emirates

The capital of the emirate of the same name, bordering Dubai. UNESCO named Sharjah the Cultural Capital of the Arab World in 1998. Sharjah International Airport is the home base of the airline Air Arabia.
