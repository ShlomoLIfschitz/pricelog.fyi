How this page is computed

  • The prices: round trips from Tel Aviv, leaving between 16 Apr and 25 Apr 2027 and back between 22 Apr and 2 May 2027, at least 2 nights.
  • Lowest found: the lowest of all the prices collected to a destination for these dates, since collection for it began. It is a price that was found, not one on offer now.
  • The prices come from searches other people already made, so they are not every price on the market.
  • A median is shown only where at least 200 prices were collected for the holiday. Prices are in dollars, as found.
