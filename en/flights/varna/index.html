Questions & answers

Flights to Varna: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Varna, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Varna?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Varna is $257, based on 802 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $130.

One way, the median is $124 and the lowest 10% are under $63 (635 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Varna?

The prices collected include direct flights: 307 of 802 round-trip prices and 276 of 635 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $184.

One way, the median of direct flights is $92.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Varna?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Varna takes about 2 hr 30 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Varna and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Varna, Bulgaria

A major port city on the Black Sea coast: the Sea Garden along the shore, the cathedral in the city center, and Roman baths among the largest in the Balkans. The resort of Zlatni Pyasatsi ("Golden Sands") is to the north along the coast.
