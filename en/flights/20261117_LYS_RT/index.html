About the destination

Lyon, France

A city in eastern France where the Rhone meets the Saone, founded as the Roman colony of Lugdunum in 43 BC. The historic center is a World Heritage Site, and the 19th-century Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourviere stands on Fourviere hill. The Lumiere brothers filmed one of the first motion pictures in the city in 1895.
