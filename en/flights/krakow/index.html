Flights to Krakow: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Krakow, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Krakow?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Krakow is $196, based on 1,663 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $126.

One way, the median is $99 and the lowest 10% are under $64 (498 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Krakow?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $134, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Krakow?

The prices collected include direct flights: 1,001 of 1,663 round-trip prices and 272 of 498 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $145.

One way, the median of direct flights is $77.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Krakow and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Krakow, Poland

Krakow, on the Vistula River, was the capital of Poland until the end of the 16th century. The old town, Wawel Hill and the historic Jewish quarter of Kazimierz are listed together as a World Heritage Site. The Auschwitz-Birkenau memorial site is about an hour's drive from the city.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
