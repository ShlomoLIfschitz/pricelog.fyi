About the destination

Paphos, Cyprus

A port city in southwest Cyprus. The archaeological sites of Kato Paphos, with their Roman mosaics, and Kouklia, home to the Sanctuary of Aphrodite, together form a World Heritage Site. Aphrodite's Rock, Petra tou Romiou, stands on the coast between Paphos and Limassol.
