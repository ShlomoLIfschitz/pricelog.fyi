Questions & answers

Flights to Madrid: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Madrid, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Madrid?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Madrid is $250, based on 1,097 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $184.

One way, the median is $132 and the lowest 10% are under $92 (776 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Madrid?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $207, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Madrid?

The prices collected include direct flights: 47 of 1,097 round-trip prices and 55 of 776 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Madrid?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Madrid takes about 5 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Madrid and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Madrid, Spain

The Prado Museum and the Thyssen-Bornemisza Museum along the Paseo del Prado, and the Reina Sofia Museum close by. The Paseo and Retiro Park have been a World Heritage Site since 2021. The Royal Palace and the Plaza Mayor are in the city center.

  • Direct flight: about 5 hr 15 min
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
