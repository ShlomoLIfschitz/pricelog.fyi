Questions & answers

Flights to Zanzibar: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Zanzibar, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Zanzibar?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Zanzibar is $927, based on 297 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $829.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Zanzibar?

The prices collected include direct flights: 65 of 297 round-trip prices and 47 of 157 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Zanzibar and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Zanzibar, Tanzania

The main city of the Zanzibar archipelago, part of Tanzania, on the west coast of Unguja island. The old town, Stone Town, is a World Heritage Site. Its Old Fort was built by the Omanis, and Freddie Mercury was born in the city in 1946.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
