Questions & answers

Flights to Bangkok: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Bangkok, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Bangkok?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Bangkok is $843, based on 726 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $712.

One way, the median is $449 and the lowest 10% are under $399 (418 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Bangkok?

The prices collected include direct flights: 5 of 726 round-trip prices and 3 of 418 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Bangkok and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Bangkok, Thailand

The capital of Thailand lies on the Chao Phraya River. Inside the Grand Palace is Wat Phra Kaew, with the Emerald Buddha, and nearby Wat Pho has a reclining Buddha 151 feet long. Wat Arun stands on the other bank of the river.
