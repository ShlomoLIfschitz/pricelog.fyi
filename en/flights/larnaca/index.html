Questions & answers

Flights to Larnaca: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Larnaca, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Larnaca?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Larnaca is $107, based on 5,475 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $64.

One way, the median is $58 and the lowest 10% are under $41 (2,339 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Larnaca?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $75, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $53.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Larnaca?

The prices collected include direct flights: 5,294 of 5,475 round-trip prices and 2,301 of 2,339 one-way prices.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Larnaca?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Larnaca takes about 1 hr. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Larnaca and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Larnaca, Cyprus

One of the closest EU coastal cities to Israel, and home to Cyprus's main international airport. The Finikoudes promenade along the beach, the ninth-century Church of Saint Lazarus, and a salt lake where flamingos spend the winter.
