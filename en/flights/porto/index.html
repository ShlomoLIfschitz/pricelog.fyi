Questions & answers

Flights to Porto: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Porto, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Porto?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Porto is $285, based on 339 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $211.

One way, the median is $114 and the lowest 10% are under $93 (227 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Porto?

The prices collected include direct flights: 3 of 339 round-trip prices and 2 of 227 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Porto and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Porto, Portugal

The second-largest city in Portugal, on the Douro River near its mouth on the Atlantic. The historic center is a World Heritage Site, and the Dom Luis I Bridge, opened in 1886, links it to Vila Nova de Gaia, home to the port wine cellars.
