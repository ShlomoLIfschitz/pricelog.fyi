Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Reykjavik and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.
