Questions & answers

Flights to Milan: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Milan, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Milan?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Milan is $142, based on 5,441 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $118.

One way, the median is $72 and the lowest 10% are under $63 (2,285 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Milan?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $125, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $66.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Milan?

The prices collected include direct flights: 3,978 of 5,441 round-trip prices and 1,931 of 2,285 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $131.

One way, the median of direct flights is $69.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Milan?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Milan takes about 4 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Milan and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Milan, Italy

The Duomo and the Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, the La Scala opera house, and Leonardo's "The Last Supper" at Santa Maria delle Grazie, a World Heritage Site. The town of Como, on the lake shore, is about 40 minutes from Milan by direct train.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
