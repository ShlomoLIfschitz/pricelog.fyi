Flights to Phuket: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Phuket, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Phuket?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Phuket is $903, based on 1,860 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $744.

One way, the median is $484 and the lowest 10% are under $417 (1,043 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Phuket?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in February 2027, the median is $829, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $469.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Phuket?

The prices collected include direct flights: 3 of 1,860 round-trip prices and 6 of 1,043 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Phuket and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Phuket, Thailand

The largest island in Thailand, in the Andaman Sea. Patong, Karon and Kata beaches are on the island's west side. A white Buddha statue 148 feet tall stands on Nakkerd Hill, and Phuket Old Town has whole streets of Sino-Portuguese houses.
