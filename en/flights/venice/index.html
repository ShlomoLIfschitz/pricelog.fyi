Questions & answers

Flights to Venice: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Venice, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Venice?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Venice is $195, based on 1,529 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $121.

One way, the median is $93 and the lowest 10% are under $66 (818 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Venice?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $134, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $73.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Venice?

The prices collected include direct flights: 596 of 1,529 round-trip prices and 305 of 818 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $127.

One way, the median of direct flights is $69.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Venice?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Venice takes about 4 hr. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Venice and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Venice, Italy

A city built on more than a hundred islands in a lagoon, and together with the lagoon a World Heritage Site: the Grand Canal, the Rialto Bridge, St. Mark's Square and the Doge's Palace. The islands of Murano and Burano are a ride away on the vaporetto, the city's water bus.

  • Direct flight: about 4 hr
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
