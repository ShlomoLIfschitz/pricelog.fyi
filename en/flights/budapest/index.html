Questions & answers

Flights to Budapest: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Budapest, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Budapest?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Budapest is $173, based on 5,163 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $131.

One way, the median is $81 and the lowest 10% are under $67 (1,488 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Budapest?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $133, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $78.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Budapest?

The prices collected include direct flights: 3,531 of 5,163 round-trip prices and 1,104 of 1,488 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $153.

One way, the median of direct flights is $73.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Budapest?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Budapest takes about 3 hr 30 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Budapest and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Budapest, Hungary

The Buda Castle Quarter and stretches of the Danube banks are part of a World Heritage Site. The Parliament Building stands on the Pest bank, and the Chain Bridge crosses the river. Thermal baths on hot springs, among them Szechenyi and Gellert, and the Dohany Street Synagogue, the largest in Europe.

  • Direct flight: about 3 hr 30 min
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
