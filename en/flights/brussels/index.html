Questions & answers

Flights to Brussels: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Brussels, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Brussels?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Brussels is $311, based on 452 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $232.

One way, the median is $141 and the lowest 10% are under $102 (305 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Brussels?

The prices collected include direct flights: 1 of 452 round-trip prices and 1 of 305 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Brussels and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Brussels, Belgium

The capital of Belgium, and home to central institutions of the European Union, among them the European Commission, and to the headquarters of NATO. The central square, the Grand-Place, is a World Heritage Site, and the Atomium was built for the 1958 World's Fair.
