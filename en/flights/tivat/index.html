Questions & answers

Flights to Tivat: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Tivat, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Tivat?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Tivat is $423, based on 254 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $320.

One way, the median is $205 and the lowest 10% are under $132 (340 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Tivat?

The prices collected include direct flights: 98 of 254 round-trip prices and 99 of 340 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Tivat and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Tivat, Montenegro

A coastal town on the Bay of Kotor, with one of Montenegro's two international airports. The Porto Montenegro marina was built on the site of a former naval shipyard. The old town of Kotor, a World Heritage Site, is at the inner end of the bay, beyond the Vrmac peninsula.
