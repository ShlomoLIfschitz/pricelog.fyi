Questions & answers

Flights to Athens: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Athens, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Athens?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Athens is $144, based on 5,001 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $118.

One way, the median is $72 and the lowest 10% are under $61 (1,125 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Athens?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $131, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Athens?

The prices collected include direct flights: 4,404 of 5,001 round-trip prices and 1,035 of 1,125 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $141.

One way, the median of direct flights is $71.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Athens?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Athens takes about 2 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Athens and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

Athens, Greece
