Questions & answers

Flights to Paphos: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Paphos, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Paphos?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Paphos is $213, based on 1,018 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $106.

One way, the median is $85 and the lowest 10% are under $58 (476 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Paphos?

The prices collected include direct flights: 811 of 1,018 round-trip prices and 399 of 476 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $180.

One way, the median of direct flights is $81.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Paphos?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Paphos takes about 1 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Paphos and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Paphos, Cyprus

A port city in southwest Cyprus. The archaeological sites of Kato Paphos, with their Roman mosaics, and Kouklia, home to the Sanctuary of Aphrodite, together form a World Heritage Site. Aphrodite's Rock, Petra tou Romiou, stands on the coast between Paphos and Limassol.

  • Direct flight: about 1 hr 15 min
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
