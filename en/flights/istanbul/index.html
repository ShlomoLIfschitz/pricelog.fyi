About the destination

Istanbul, Turkey

A city on both sides of the Bosphorus, in Europe and in Asia. Its historic areas, among them Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and Topkapi Palace, are a World Heritage Site. City ferries cross the strait between the two continents.
