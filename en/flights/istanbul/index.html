Questions & answers

Flights to Istanbul: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Istanbul, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Istanbul?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Istanbul is $311, based on 1,520 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $211.

One way, the median is $148 and the lowest 10% are under $94 (1,848 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Istanbul?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $280, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Istanbul?

None of the 3,368 prices collected for Istanbul were for a direct flight.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Istanbul and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Istanbul, Turkey

A city on both sides of the Bosphorus, in Europe and in Asia. Its historic areas, among them Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and Topkapi Palace, are a World Heritage Site. City ferries cross the strait between the two continents.
