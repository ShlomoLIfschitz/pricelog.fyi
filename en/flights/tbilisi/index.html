Questions & answers

Flights to Tbilisi: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Tbilisi, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Tbilisi?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Tbilisi is $224, based on 6,056 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $180.

One way, the median is $125 and the lowest 10% are under $86 (5,322 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Tbilisi?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $200, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is also November 2026, with a median of $99.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Tbilisi?

The prices collected include direct flights: 5,634 of 6,056 round-trip prices and 4,105 of 5,322 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $221.

One way, the median of direct flights is $104.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Tbilisi?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Tbilisi takes about 2 hr 30 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Tbilisi and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Tbilisi, Georgia

The capital of Georgia, on the banks of the Mtkvari (Kura) River: an old town with wooden balconies, bathhouses over sulfur springs, and Narikala Fortress on the ridge above it. Mtskheta, the ancient capital and a World Heritage Site, is about 12 miles northwest of Tbilisi.
