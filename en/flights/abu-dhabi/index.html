Questions & answers

Flights to Abu Dhabi: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Abu Dhabi, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Abu Dhabi?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Abu Dhabi is $591, based on 489 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $455.

One way, the median is $288 and the lowest 10% are under $194 (662 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Abu Dhabi?

The prices collected include direct flights: 79 of 489 round-trip prices and 52 of 662 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Abu Dhabi and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates

The capital of the United Arab Emirates, on an island off the Persian Gulf coast. The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque was completed in 2007, and the Louvre Abu Dhabi, designed by Jean Nouvel, opened on Saadiyat Island in 2017. The Qasr Al Watan presidential palace has been open to visitors since 2019.
