Questions & answers

Flights to Warsaw: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Warsaw, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Warsaw?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Warsaw is $186, based on 1,289 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $137.

One way, the median is $96 and the lowest 10% are under $71 (551 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Warsaw?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in November 2026, the median is $154, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Warsaw?

The prices collected include direct flights: 686 of 1,289 round-trip prices and 310 of 551 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $149.

One way, the median of direct flights is $80.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Warsaw and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Warsaw, Poland

The capital of Poland, on the Vistula River. The old town, destroyed in World War II and rebuilt, is a World Heritage Site. POLIN Museum of the History of Polish Jews stands opposite the Monument to the Ghetto Heroes.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
