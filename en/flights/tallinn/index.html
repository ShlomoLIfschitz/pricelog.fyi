The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Tallinn is $296, based on 267 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $241.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Tallinn?

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Tallinn and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Tallinn, Estonia

The capital of Estonia, on the Gulf of Finland. The old town, with its Gothic town hall completed in 1404, is a World Heritage Site. Helsinki is about 50 miles to the north, across the gulf.
