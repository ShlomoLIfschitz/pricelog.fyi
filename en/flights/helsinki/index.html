Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Helsinki and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Helsinki, Finland

The capital of Finland, on the Gulf of Finland. The cathedral, on Senate Square, was completed in 1852, and the Suomenlinna sea fortress, on islands at the harbor entrance, is a World Heritage Site. Tallinn is about 50 miles to the south, across the gulf.
