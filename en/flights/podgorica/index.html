Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Podgorica and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Podgorica, Montenegro

The capital and largest city of Montenegro, where the Ribnica River meets the Moraca. The Ottoman clock tower stands in its old town, Stara Varos, and the city's airport is one of Montenegro's two international airports; the other is in Tivat.
