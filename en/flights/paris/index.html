Questions & answers

Flights to Paris: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Paris, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Paris?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Paris is $315, based on 5,624 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $218.

One way, the median is $129 and the lowest 10% are under $98 (2,270 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Paris?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in March 2027, the median is $222, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $106.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Paris?

The prices collected include direct flights: 34 of 5,624 round-trip prices and 58 of 2,270 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Paris?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Paris takes about 5 hr. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Paris and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Paris, France

The Eiffel Tower, the Louvre, Notre-Dame and the Marais. The historic stretch of the banks of the Seine in the city center is a World Heritage Site. The Palace of Versailles, also a World Heritage Site, is in the town of Versailles, southwest of Paris.
