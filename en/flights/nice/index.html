Flights to Nice: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Nice, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Nice?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Nice is $318, based on 228 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $245.

One way, the median is $127 and the lowest 10% are under $107 (212 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Nice?

The prices collected include direct flights: 2 of 228 round-trip prices and 0 of 212 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Nice and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Nice, France

A city on the Mediterranean coast of the French Riviera. The Promenade des Anglais runs along the Bay of Angels, and since 2021 the city has been a World Heritage Site as the winter resort town of the Riviera. Museums dedicated to Henri Matisse and Marc Chagall are in the city.
