Questions & answers

Flights to Rome: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Rome, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Rome?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Rome is $132, based on 8,151 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $116.

One way, the median is $70 and the lowest 10% are under $63 (2,423 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Rome?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $120, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $67.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Rome?

The prices collected include direct flights: 6,967 of 8,151 round-trip prices and 2,070 of 2,423 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $129.

One way, the median of direct flights is $68.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Rome?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Rome takes about 3 hr 45 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Rome and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Rome, Italy

The Colosseum and the Roman Forum, the Pantheon, Piazza Navona and the Trevi Fountain, and across the Tiber the Trastevere neighborhood. Vatican City, with St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel, is a separate country inside the city.
