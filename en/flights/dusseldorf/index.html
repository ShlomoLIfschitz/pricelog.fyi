How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Dusseldorf?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Dusseldorf is $312, based on 368 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $239.

One way, the median is $119 and the lowest 10% are under $98 (307 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Dusseldorf?

The prices collected include direct flights: 15 of 368 round-trip prices and 10 of 307 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Dusseldorf and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Dusseldorf, Germany

The capital of the state of North Rhine-Westphalia, on the Rhine. The three Neuer Zollhof buildings in the Media Harbor were designed by Frank Gehry and completed in 1999, and the Rhine Tower, the city's telecommunications tower, is about 790 feet tall. The poet Heinrich Heine was born in the city in 1797.
