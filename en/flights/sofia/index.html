Flights to Sofia: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Sofia, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Sofia?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Sofia is $197, based on 1,217 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $167.

One way, the median is $99 and the lowest 10% are under $86 (422 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Sofia?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $181, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Sofia?

The prices collected include direct flights: 722 of 1,217 round-trip prices and 256 of 422 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $189.

One way, the median of direct flights is $95.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Sofia and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Sofia, Bulgaria

The capital of Bulgaria, at the foot of Mount Vitosha. The Alexander Nevsky Cathedral was built between 1882 and 1912, and the Boyana Church, on the edge of the city, is a World Heritage Site. The Sofia Synagogue, inaugurated in 1909, is the largest Sephardic synagogue in Europe.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
