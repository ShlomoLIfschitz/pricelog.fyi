Flights to Riga: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Riga, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Riga?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Riga is $392, based on 404 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $297.

One way, the median is $178 and the lowest 10% are under $123 (231 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Riga?

The prices collected include direct flights: 14 of 404 round-trip prices and 2 of 231 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Riga and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Riga, Latvia

The capital of Latvia, on the Daugava River, near the Gulf of Riga. The historic center, with one of the largest concentrations of Art Nouveau buildings in the world, is a World Heritage Site. The House of the Blackheads was destroyed in World War II and rebuilt in the 1990s.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
