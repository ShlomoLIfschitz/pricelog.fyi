Questions & answers

Flights to Lyon: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Lyon, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Lyon?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Lyon is $279, based on 372 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $170.

One way, the median is $125 and the lowest 10% are under $93 (203 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Lyon?

The prices collected include direct flights: 113 of 372 round-trip prices and 69 of 203 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Lyon and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Lyon, France

A city in eastern France where the Rhone meets the Saone, founded as the Roman colony of Lugdunum in 43 BC. The historic center is a World Heritage Site, and the 19th-century Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourviere stands on Fourviere hill. The Lumiere brothers filmed one of the first motion pictures in the city in 1895.

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
