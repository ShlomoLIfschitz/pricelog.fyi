Questions & answers

Flights to Toronto: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Toronto, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Toronto?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Toronto is $954, based on 495 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $819.

One way, the median is $559 and the lowest 10% are under $482 (225 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Toronto?

The prices collected include direct flights: 5 of 495 round-trip prices and 0 of 225 one-way prices.

That is fewer than 200 prices, so PriceLog does not calculate a separate median for them.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Toronto and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

Toronto, Canada
