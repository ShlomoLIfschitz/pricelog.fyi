Questions & answers

Flights to Batumi: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Batumi, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Batumi?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Batumi is $328, based on 4,989 prices collected since 11 Sep 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $185.

One way, the median is $132 and the lowest 10% are under $99 (3,135 prices).

When is the cheapest time to fly to Batumi?

Among the prices collected for round-trip flights departing in January 2027, the median is $223, the lowest of the months with at least 200 prices.

One way, it is November 2026, with a median of $123.

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Batumi?

The prices collected include direct flights: 3,522 of 4,989 round-trip prices and 2,499 of 3,135 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $284.

One way, the median of direct flights is $125.

How long is the flight from Tel Aviv to Batumi?

A direct flight from Tel Aviv to Batumi takes about 2 hr 15 min. That is the median length of the one-way direct flights found, rounded to the quarter hour.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Batumi and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Batumi, Georgia

Georgia's port city on the Black Sea: a pebble beach along a long boulevard, and the moving sculpture "Ali and Nino." The Batumi Botanical Garden is at Green Cape, north of the city.
