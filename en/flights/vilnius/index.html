Questions & answers

Flights to Vilnius: questions and answers

Each answer is calculated from the prices collected for Vilnius, and updates along with them.

How much is a flight from Tel Aviv to Vilnius?

The median price of a round-trip flight from Tel Aviv to Vilnius is $229, based on 596 prices collected since 11 Oct 2026. The lowest 10% of prices are under $148.

One way, the median is $96 and the lowest 10% are under $74 (398 prices).

Is there a direct flight from Tel Aviv to Vilnius?

The prices collected include direct flights: 292 of 596 round-trip prices and 252 of 398 one-way prices.

Round trip, the median of direct flights is $170.

One way, the median of direct flights is $86.

Where do the prices on this page come from?

Every 6 hours, PriceLog collects prices for flights from Tel Aviv to Vilnius and keeps each one as it was found. Every price was found in a search someone already made, so the prices do not include every flight that exists. The final price is set on the booking site.

The numbers are based on prices collected, not on every flight that exists.

About the destination

Vilnius, Lithuania

The capital of Lithuania. The old town, with Baroque churches, is a World Heritage Site. Gediminas' Tower, on a hill above the Neris and Vilnia rivers, overlooks the cathedral and the Palace of the Grand Dukes. In the 19th century the city was called "the Jerusalem of Lithuania."

  • UNESCO World Heritage Site
