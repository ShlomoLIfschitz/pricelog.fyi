Privacy

Privacy policy

This page describes what pricelog.fyi does with information about you. It is short because the site does very little.

What this website collects

Nothing. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising network, and no JavaScript of any kind on this site. No cookies are set. No fonts, scripts, images, or stylesheets are loaded from anyone else’s server, so no third party learns that you visited. There is no account, no login, and no form to fill in.

What the host necessarily sees

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. To deliver a page to you at all, Cloudflare receives your IP address, the page you asked for, your browser’s user-agent string, and the time of the request, and records these in standard server logs used for delivery, abuse prevention, and aggregate traffic counts. I can see aggregate request numbers in Cloudflare’s dashboard. I cannot see who you are, and I do not attempt to identify individual visitors. Cloudflare processes this data as my hosting provider under its own terms.

What happens when you follow a link

This is the part that actually matters, so it is not buried.

Links to products are affiliate links. When you click one, you leave this site and arrive at AliExpress, and AliExpress records that you arrived through my affiliate identifier — typically by setting a cookie in your browser. That is how a commission is attributed if you later buy something. Once you have left this site, AliExpress’s own privacy policy governs what happens, not this one.

The same applies to the Telegram link. Joining the channel is a relationship between you and Telegram, under Telegram’s privacy policy. As the channel owner I can see the total subscriber count and how many people joined through a given invite link. I cannot see the list of individual subscribers, and I do not receive your phone number or contact details.

If you email me

Mail sent to contact@pricelog.fyi is forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing to a Gmail mailbox, so your message and your address are handled by Cloudflare and Google in transit and at rest. I keep correspondence only for as long as it is useful to answer you. Ask me to delete an email exchange and I will.

Your rights

Because the site holds no records about you, there is nothing here to access, correct, or export. If you have emailed me, you may ask what I hold and ask me to delete it, at the address above.

Changes

If this site ever starts collecting something — analytics, for example — this page will say so before it does, and the date below will change. It is not a document I intend to quietly expand.
